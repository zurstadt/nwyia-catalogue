# Fonds P. Nwyia — *Index Lieux*: Provenance and Identification Notes

Working notes compiled during the digital adjudication of Paul Nwyia's
*Index Lieux* (the manuscript catalogue of the Fonds P. Nwyia, Bibliothèque
Orientale, Université Saint-Joseph, Beirut). They record catalogue
identifications, shelfmark resolutions, and provenance corrections intended for
the forthcoming publication of these findings. Manuscript references below are
keyed to the project's row ids (`data/data.json`).

---

## 1. The "Tübingen" manuscripts are Berlin holdings consulted in the *Tübinger Depot*

**Finding.** Eleven manuscripts that Nwyia records as held at **Tübingen** —
all copies of Ibn ʿAṭāʾ Allāh al-Iskandarī's *al-Ḥikam al-ʿAṭāʾiyya*, cited by
bare number (159, 275, 552, 553, 889, 1169, 1172, 1730, 1757, 1771, 1982) — are
in fact manuscripts of the **Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin (formerly Preußische
Staatsbibliothek)**. Nwyia, working in the 1960s, recorded the *location at
which he consulted them*: the **Tübinger Depot der Preußischen
Staatsbibliothek**, the wartime/post-war repository of the Berlin oriental
collections at the Universitätsbibliothek Tübingen.

**Historical background.** During the Second World War the holdings of the
Preußische Staatsbibliothek were evacuated from Berlin to some thirty locations.
The orientalist then in charge, **Max Weisweiler**, had the bulk of the oriental
manuscripts sent *west* rather than east — first to the abbeys of Banz and
Beuron (the latter in the French occupation zone) — whence, in **1948**, they
were transferred to the **Universitätsbibliothek Tübingen** and constituted the
so-called **"Tübinger Depot der Preußischen Staatsbibliothek."** The depot was
**dissolved in 1968**, the manuscripts returning to West Berlin (Staatsbibliothek
Preußischer Kulturbesitz) on the opening of its new building. (A separate portion
of the collection passed to Marburg and thence likewise to West Berlin; another
remained in / returned to the Deutsche Staatsbibliothek in East Berlin; some
1,500 manuscripts never left Berlin; and a further tranche — the "Berlinka" —
remains in Kraków.) A scholar consulting these manuscripts in Tübingen between
1948 and 1968 would naturally have recorded their location as "Tübingen."

That the eleven bare numbers are **Berlin shelfmark numbers** (and not Tübingen
"Ma VI" shelfmarks — whose Arabic series stops near no. 382) is confirmed against
the union catalogue **Qalamos**: every one resolves to a Staatsbibliothek zu
Berlin record (`owner_de1`), all belonging to the *al-Ḥikam* group catalogued by
Ahlwardt under no. 8690. Fittingly, the Qalamos record for Landberg 552 cites
Nwyia's own 1986 edition of the *Ḥikam*.

## 2. Related identification corrections (selected)

Other corrections established during the same adjudication, for cross-reference:

- **r0074 — *Ādāb al-aqṭāb*.** Nwyia's "Maḥmūd al-Kāzawānī" is a misreading; the
  author is **ʿAlī b. Aḥmad b. Muḥammad al-Ḥamawī al-Shādhilī al-Kīzwānī**, the
  work a *taṣawwuf/samāʿ* text. Witness: Berlin Sprenger 851, ff. 177a–177b
  (Ahlwardt 3199). Identified via *al-Maṣādir al-ʿarabiyya li-l-mūsīqā*
  (saramusik.org, no. 320).
- **Vatican vs. BnF mislabelling.** A normalization fault had assigned several
  *Vaticani arabi* (and one Escorial) manuscript to the Bibliothèque nationale de
  France because they share the "Arabe"/"Syriaque" shelfmark prefix. Corrected:
  e.g. the al-Qūnawī cluster (Vat. ar. 291, 297, 1397, 1428, 1436, 1453) and
  Ibn ʿAbbād al-Rundī (El Escorial, árabe 1758).
- **BnF title corrections.** Arabe 6630 is al-Ghazālī's *Tahāfut al-falāsifa*
  (Nwyia: *Tahāfut al-tahāfut*); Arabe 6819 is Mullā Ṣadrā's *al-Asfār
  al-arbaʿa* (Nwyia: *al-Asrār al-arbaʿa*).
- **Vat. ar. 1436.** The text at ff. 82a–85a is *Risālat manāzil al-abdāl fī
  bayān al-maqāmāt wa-l-aḥwāl* of al-Qūnawī (Nwyia read *manāzil al-ʿibāda*),
  confirmed from the manuscript heading.
