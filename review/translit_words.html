Word transliteration

One Arabic word at a time. Type its transliteration, press Enter. Words are ordered by how many titles they unlock, so the early ones pay for themselves.

The main app's shorthand works here: a:ā i:ī u:ū · h.ḥ s.ṣ d.ḍ t.ṭ z.ẓ · d_ḏ t_ṯ g_ġ h_ḫ s_š j_ǧ · 'ʾ `ʿ

Titles in this corpus are lowercase — write kitāb, not Kitāb.

If a word needs different forms in different titles — a construct qaṣīdat beside an absolute qaṣīda — tick varies by context. Titles containing it are then left for you to write out, rather than composed from the word list.

Nothing here touches the catalogue. Export writes a word list; a separate step composes the titles and files them as suggestions, which you accept or override in the main app — so composed titles stay distinguishable from ones you wrote.
