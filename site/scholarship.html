Scholarship

Not every item in the Fonds is a premodern manuscript. The collection also gathered modern scholarly materials — Paul Nwyia's own studies and translations, the work of colleagues such as Michel Allard, and editions by George Makdisi and Hellmut Ritter, alongside a few modern authors. These are kept here, apart from the manuscript catalogue and excluded from its counts.
