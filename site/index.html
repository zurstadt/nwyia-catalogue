Provenance recovered

The Tübingen Depot

Eleven copies of Ibn ʿAṭāʾ Allāh's Ḥikam that Nwyia recorded as held at “Tübingen” are in fact Berlin manuscripts: the oriental holdings of the Preußische Staatsbibliothek were displaced to a depot at Tübingen between 1945 and 1968. Each has been recoded to its true Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin shelfmark and linked to Qalamos.

This and other identification corrections — Vatican and Escorial mislabels, BnF title fixes — are documented in the project's findings.

A note on the index

Nwyia's archive numbers refer to his own ordering of the collection, not to a holding library. Works are therefore identified by author and title rather than by shelfmark alone — the principle behind this catalogue.
